ISDS web service definitions (v2.0), unmodified copies of the public files.

Source:        https://www.datovka.gov.cz/static/wsdl/v20/
Downloaded:    2026-11-24

Files and the service each WSDL is bound to (soap:address):
  dm_operations.wsdl  https://ws1.datovka.gov.cz/DS/dz        CreateMessage, SignedSentMessageDownload
  dm_info.wsdl        https://ws1.datovka.gov.cz/DS/dx        GetDeliveryInfo, GetSignedDeliveryInfo
  db_access.wsdl      https://ws1.datovka.gov.cz/DS/DsManage  GetOwnerInfoFromLogin2, GetUserInfoFromLogin2
  dmBaseTypes.xsd     imported by dm_operations.wsdl and dm_info.wsdl
  dbTypes.xsd         imported by db_access.wsdl

The test environment serves the same paths on https://ws1.datovka-test.gov.cz.
The userPrivils bit values (0x4 create and send, 0x8 view info, ...) are not in
the WSDL; they come from the isds_privileges enum of libdatovka
(https://gitlab.nic.cz/datovka/libdatovka, include/libdatovka/isds.h).
